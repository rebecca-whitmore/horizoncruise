# Horizon Cruise: core page SEO

**Prepared: 11 November 2026**  
**Website: https://horizoncruise.co.uk**  
**Scope: homepage, About page and Contact page**

This is the consolidated SEO copy for the three core pages. It records the direction agreed in conversation and adds a newly recommended homepage H1. It does not change the website or approve a wider navigation structure.

## 1. Homepage

| Element | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Overall SEO focus | Cruise holiday guides, advice and inspiration |
| Primary keyword phrase | Cruise holiday guides |
| Supporting themes | Cruise advice; cruise inspiration; cruise-and-stay guidance |
| Audience | UK holidaymakers, including newcomers and existing cruisers |
| SEO title | Horizon Cruise \| Cruise Holiday Guides, Advice & Inspiration |
| H1 — newly recommended | Cruise holiday guides to help you choose with confidence |
| URL | https://horizoncruise.co.uk/ |

### Meta description

Explore cruise holiday guides, practical advice and fresh inspiration. Horizon Cruise helps you picture the whole holiday and find what suits you.

### Page direction

- Introduce a worldwide cruise publication with particular expertise in cruise-and-stay and understanding the whole holiday.
- Reflect the brand principle: our expertise helps readers picture the experience and recognise whether it suits them.
- Include discovery and inspiration alongside practical help.
- Alaska and Asia may feature through early articles; they do not define the site's overall scope or homepage keyword focus.
- Build readership and newsletter subscriptions. There is no booking enquiry service at launch.

## 2. About page

| Element | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Primary keyword phrase | About Horizon Cruise |
| Supporting themes | UK cruise publication; cruise expertise; editorial approach |
| SEO title | About Horizon Cruise \| Our Publication & Approach |
| H1 | About Horizon Cruise |
| Suggested URL | https://horizoncruise.co.uk/about/ |

### Meta description

Discover Horizon Cruise, a publication for UK holidaymakers combining cruise expertise, researched guidance and traveller stories to help you choose confidently.

### Page direction

Explain who HC serves, what makes its guidance useful, and how cruise expertise, verified research and traveller contributions inform its coverage. Keep statements about experience, authorship and editorial responsibility accurate. The publication leads publicly; do not imply an invented team or universal first-hand experience.

## 3. Contact page

| Element | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Primary keyword phrase | Contact Horizon Cruise |
| Supporting themes | Editorial enquiries; reader feedback; commercial enquiries |
| SEO title | Contact Horizon Cruise \| Enquiries & Feedback |
| H1 | Contact Horizon Cruise |
| Suggested URL | https://horizoncruise.co.uk/contact/ |

### Meta description

Get in touch with Horizon Cruise to share feedback, suggest a story or discuss an editorial or commercial enquiry. We welcome hearing from our readers.

### Page direction

Provide a clear contact method and explain the enquiries welcomed. Make clear that HC is not currently handling cruise bookings. Do not promise response times that have not been agreed.

## 4. Implementation notes for the Technical and Writing Leads

- The SEO title is the page's title metadata. The H1 is its main visible heading. Use the distinct wording supplied above for each purpose.
- Use the meta descriptions exactly as supplied unless Rebecca approves revisions. Ensure the visible page content supports their promises, particularly traveller stories on the About page.
- Supporting themes guide natural copy; they are not phrases to repeat mechanically. The homepage's overall focus is not one long exact-match keyword.
- About and Contact focus on branded searches and their page purposes rather than broad cruise subjects.
- The proposed About and Contact URLs are for new pages. Check any existing routes before implementation; this document does not authorise URL changes or redirects.
- In the headless setup, verify that the intended metadata appears on the public frontend, with no conflicting duplicate titles or descriptions and with canonical URLs identifying the public HC pages. This is a verification requirement, not a diagnosed fault.

**Evidence status:** These keyword phrases express the agreed editorial direction and page purposes. UK search demand and comparative keyword difficulty have not been validated. No ranking or traffic outcome is promised.
